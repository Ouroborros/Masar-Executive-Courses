# Canva homepage candidates — 15 September 2026

Sixteen homepage mock-ups were generated in Canva (desktop, 1920 × 1080,
above the fold) across five directions, then each was created as a design
and viewed. The Canva account held no website designs, brand templates or
brand kits of its own, so the candidates were generated from the ledger
brief in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

Judging bar: the live ledger site (v9 artifact) and the reference set in
`premium-editorial-references.md` and `exec-ed-school-sites.md` — one
accent, typographic restraint, dense information, hairline rules, no stock
photography, both scripts.

## Directions generated

| Direction | Brief | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Dark ledger | The live identity: ink ground, off-white text, oxblood accent, serif display, ledger table | Strongest as a family. Two of the four in the best five. |
| Light paper | Same identity on the site's light-mode ground | Two in the best five. Confirms light mode is a full peer, not a fallback. |
| Band-led editorial | Oxblood band carrying the headline, three-column zone below | One in the best five; the other three drift to bright crimson or pink. |
| Arabic-first RTL | مسار wordmark, naskh-style display, RTL ledger | None selected. Canva reached for kufi display faces, a device mock-up and a stock book photo. The live site's Markazi Text treatment stays. |
| Corporate blue cards (control) | Blue header, search hero, four cards | None selected. All four look like a generic training aggregator, which is the look the ledger was chosen to avoid. |

## Best five

Ranked against the live site and the reference bar. Canva design IDs are in
the user's account under the folder "Masar website — best 5".

1. **Sophisticated Masar Magazine** (`DAHVPn2m4nM`, band-led). The only
   candidate that covers the full index-page anatomy: masthead, oxblood
   headline band, then a three-column zone of schools, featured programmes
   and lists. Adopt the three-column zone below the fold. Fix: gold text
   should be the off-white ink; the nav duplicates "Courses" and "Schools".
2. **Premium Homepage Mockup** (`DAHVPsaqF94`, dark ledger). The closest
   table treatment to the live index: column headers Title, Dates, School,
   Days, Fee; hairline rows; fees right-aligned. Fix: the headline is set in
   a sans and should be Newsreader.
3. **Serene Ledger-Style Homepage** (`DAHVPn10AGk`, light paper). The best
   light-mode record page: label left, value right, hairlines, warm paper.
   Almost identical to the live record page in light mode. Fix: "Menu"
   should read "Courses".
4. **Tactile Editorial Homepage** (`DAHVPg1GwBg`, dark ledger). Complete
   masthead (Courses, Schools, Lists, Saved, EN | AR) and a record page
   with right-aligned values. Fix: the decorative display serif should be
   Newsreader; the wordmark should be serif.
5. **Minimalist Editorial Homepage** (`DAHVPghbEkM`, light paper). The
   purest identity statement: serif wordmark, headline with an oxblood
   rule, hairline ledger. Fix: rows are empty; fill with real records.

Near misses: `DAHVPmpspG0` (dark, cream table panel is off-system),
`DAHVPl6crKQ` (best of the Arabic set, but kufi display type).

## Conclusion

The live ledger direction holds. Canva's own attempts at alternatives
(bright-red bands, blue card grids) read as generic; the candidates that
score highest are the ones closest to what is already built. Two things
are worth taking from the exercise:

- a three-column "schools / featured / lists" zone below the fold on the
  index page, from candidate 1;
- confidence that light mode is a full peer of the dark ledger, from
  candidates 3 and 5.
