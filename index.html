How programmes get here

Facts from the schools. Nothing invented.

Masar reads the programme pages the schools publish and records what they say — title, dates, teaching days, format, language and fee. That is all a card shows, and all it claims.

  1. Taken from the source

    Listings come from each school's own published calendar and are refreshed regularly.

  2. No ratings we did not earn

    Imported programmes carry no star ratings, testimonials or marketing copy. If a school did not state it, the card does not either.

  3. No paid placement

    Position on this site is not for sale. Collections are editorial, and say so.

  4. Straight to the school

    An enquiry goes to the institution running the programme. Your details are not resold.
