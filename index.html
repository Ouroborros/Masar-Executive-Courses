§04

Methodالمنهج

How a programme gets into the index, and what we will not do to it.

We list. We do not sell.

01

Source

Every record is taken from the school's own published programme page. If a school did not state it, this index does not state it either.

02

Currency

The fee is the school's own, in the school's own currency. We do not convert the figure we publish.

03

Silence

An unrecorded field prints an em dash. An index that fills its own gaps is not an index.

04

Position

No rankings, no ratings, no featured slots, no paid placement. Order is by date, fee, teaching days or school — whichever you choose.

USDCHFCADSGDAUDDKKEUR

Seven currencies appear in this index because seven currencies appear on the schools' own pages. Converting them would be a guess printed as a fact.

Beside every fee we also print an indicative riyal figure and the date of the rate we used. It is an indication for a budget line, not a quote.

الرقم كما نشرته الكلية. لا تحويل، ولا تقدير.
